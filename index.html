OUR STORY

About Kanikaara

Grace in Gold, Stories Untold

Kanikaara was born from a passion for preserving India's rich jewellery heritage while making it accessible to every woman who deserves to shine. Founded in Jaipur — the Pink City and the heart of India's gemstone trade — we blend centuries of Rajasthani artistry with modern sensibilities.

The name Kanikaara evokes grace, beauty, and timeless elegance. Every piece in our collection carries the soul of skilled artisans who have dedicated their lives to this craft — from Kundan setters in Jaipur to meenakari painters of Nathdwara.

Whether it's your wedding day, a festival, or a casual afternoon — we believe jewellery should tell your story. That's our promise: Grace in Gold, Stories Untold.
